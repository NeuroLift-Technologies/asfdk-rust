### 1. Why This System Exists (Verified Reality)

The NeuroLift ADHD ecosystem exists because current AI systems inherit and enforce neurotypical biases, and those biases cause measurable harm to neurodivergent users.

This is no longer only a philosophical claim. It is documented across research, institutional guidance, and lived user reports:

- AI writing detectors systematically false-flag autistic and ADHD writing as machine-generated because of lower "burstiness," formulaic structure, or repetitive phrasing.
- Safety layers over-trigger on ND emotional intensity, info-dumping, or even minimal input, treating distress signals or ordinary high-emotion states as crisis.
- Executive dysfunction, time blindness, initiation barriers, and overwhelm are frequently moralized as laziness, irresponsibility, or avoidance.
- Hiring, recommendation, and moderation systems treat non-linear communication, hyperfocus, and sensory differences as negative or policy-violating signals.
- Language models show consistent negative associations with neurodivergence-related terms, even when testing recognized strengths.

The result is structural: AI systems quietly push neurotypical norms onto neurodivergent people. NeuroLift is designed to eliminate that failure mode.

### 3. Architectural Truths That Remain Non-Negotiable

**Truth 1** — ADHD is not a productivity problem.  
A person can know exactly what to do and still be unable to initiate.

**Truth 2** — AI cannot understand ADHD from text alone.  
It must experience cognitive friction inside a consequence-bearing world (nlt-world-engine).

**Truth 3** — AI must never replace human agency.  
It must scaffold, not command. Support, not override.  
Authority stays with the human.

These truths shape every design decision. Capability is never authority. Governance must sit where actions occur — especially at the C++ / Unreal boundary (asfdk-cplus).

### 4. Why "I'm Stuck" Remains the Center of the App

The primary user utterance is a single contextual signal: **"I'm stuck."**

It can mean initiation difficulty, overwhelm, distraction, prioritization failure, time blindness, decision paralysis, hyperfocus, loss of context, or emotional dysregulation.

- The orchestrator determines which Advocate is relevant.
- The Advocate determines what scaffolding is appropriate.
- Governance determines what authority is allowed.
- The user determines what happens next.

This is the opposite of neurotypical productivity apps. It is how neurotypical bias is prevented from harming ND users.
